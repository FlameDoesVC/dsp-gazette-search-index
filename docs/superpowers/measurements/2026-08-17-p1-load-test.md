## Planner check

`EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` for `vector_en @@ websearch_to_tsquery('english',
'ministry notice')`:

- gazette partition: **Bitmap Index Scan** on `search_searchdocument_gazette_vector_en_idx`
  (the clone of `sd_vec_en_gin`) — partial-index predicate matches.
- ibay partition: sequential scan, a correct planner decision at that
  selectivity (`ministry` matched ~70k of the 120k synthetic rows); selective
  terms in the real corpus use the GIN index.

## Postgres settings in force

- `shared_buffers` = 128 MB
- `work_mem` = 4 MB

## Projection check (spec 12.6)

Spec 12.6 projects roughly 670 MB total at 51,000 iulaan. At 120,751 mixed
documents we measured 186 MB including the synthetic English vocabulary. The
synthetic corpus is entirely English with a small Zipf vocabulary, so it is a
lower bound for a Dhivehi-heavy gazette corpus; the projection is not refuted
but is not directly comparable because the synthetic set lacks Thaana lexemes
(the Dhivehi GIN vectors are near-empty at this point).
